Tetris

Slide on the well, or use the keys along the bottom. Left and right move. A short tap on the well, or the curved arrow, turns the piece. The single chevron is a soft drop, a downward flick or the double chevron drops it straight down, and H parks the piece once.

Clear a full line to score. Every ten lines the well gets quicker. The round arrow starts a fresh well.
